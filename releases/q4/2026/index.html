Client communicationReleasing in Q4

Auto-Logging Client Communication

Every call, text and email with a client is logged automatically, in one timeline.

Until now, client conversations lived in three places: calls in one RingCentral tab, texts in another, and email in a shared Gmail inbox. Calls weren’t recorded, so when something was missed, nobody could check what was actually said.

Auto-logging puts every conversation on one timeline for each client, records and transcribes every call, and feeds the same history into Pipeline.

Landing Pages

Build the whole page inside riseOS, with tracking and booking built in.

Campaign Builder, the first version, gave you a form to embed on a page you already had. Landing Pages replaces it with the whole page: built in riseOS from proven templates, tracked automatically, and booking the visit on the spot.

Program Tracker

See every client’s program in one place: whether they’re on track, which visits didn’t match the plan, how many visits are remaining, and what’s on the program schedule.

Programs are tracked today in a Google Sheet that isn’t connected to Zenoti, so every visit gets typed in twice. Program Tracker syncs with Zenoti and opens each client’s full program in one panel.

The panel shows whether the client is on track, how closely their visits match the plan, flags any visit that didn’t, and keeps track of every service they still need to make up.

PerformanceIn beta

Goal Dashboard

Set your monthly goals once, and riseOS tells you every day whether each number is on track or off track, and exactly how much you need per day to catch up.

The Goal Dashboard measures today’s numbers against the goal you set for the month: leads, arrivals, programs sold, repurchases and revenue. It shows whether you’re on pace today, not just at the end of the month. It syncs overnight, so it’s always one day behind.

Goals are set in Settings › Goal Setting. Each month starts from your last full month’s numbers, you adjust what you want to change, and the goal locks in once the month begins.

Loading the walkthrough
See How It WorksStep 1 of 12
The dashboard

Four targets across the top, a chart of the month so far and revenue to date. Every number is benchmarked against this month’s goal.

On track or off track

Each card shows whether you’re on track for where you should be today. Arrivals and repurchases are on track. Leads and programs sold are off track.

The daily number

Tap an Off track tag to see what it takes to catch up. Leads are 30 of 230, so you need 11 leads a day to hit 230 by October 31.

Day by day

Click any card and the chart switches to it. Here, programs sold for October 1 to 12: 14 so far against a target of 42.

Revenue to date

The store is at $72,000, 72% of the revenue goal. Revenue looks great, but leads are the early warning: today’s arrivals come from earlier leads, so low leads now mean fewer arrivals in a few weeks.

Set the goal in Settings

Go to Settings › Goal Setting. Each year lists its months and their status. Past months are closed and October is in progress, so neither can be changed. November and December aren’t set yet.

Start from real numbers

Click the pencil on November. Every input starts from your last full month: revenue $94,000, booking rate 65%, show rate 66%, close rate 43%, and your best month for each is shown for reference.

Raise the revenue goal

Slide the revenue goal up 10% to $103,000. The dashboard targets update with it: 262 leads, 112 arrivals and 48 programs sold.

Change what you’ll improve

Adjust any lever. Raise close rate 5% to 45.2% and you need fewer people coming in to hit the same revenue: leads drop to 251 and arrivals to 107.

Save the goal

Click Save goal. November changes from Not set to Set.

It locks when the month starts

Back in the list, November shows $103,000 and Set. Once November begins, it locks like October, so the target can’t move mid-month.

Today vs. the goal

From then on, every morning the dashboard measures that day’s numbers against the locked goal and shows you exactly where you stand.
